100 Years of Imagination

Together, we saw that...

  • As sci-fi grew as an artform, directors would use fantastical sci-fi tropes like robots and space in tandem with humanistic tropes like free will and social control to provide social commentary.
  • As time progressed, sci-fi changed from depicting large time leaps to small leaps as the intention shifted from escapism and speculation to confronting contemporary anxieties.
  • Over time, the role of distant worlds in sci-fi evolved from serving as destinations for escapist or speculative adventures to acting as allegorical settings that comment on domestic issues.
So what?

So, how did people’s attitudes towards sci-fi change over time? Originally it started as technological entertainment meant to either distract or speculate. As time went on, it progressed into an artform meant to depict contemporary social commentary through a fantastical lens.
