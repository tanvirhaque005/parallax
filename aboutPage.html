100 Years of Sci-Fi Stories

This visualization uses data from culturally significant sci-fi films across each decade. Combined with thematic research on world-building and technology in cinema, it reveals how sci-fi mirrors the world and how those visions evolve over time.

You’ll explore:
  • How sci-fi tropes shift across decades
  • How far each film looks into the future
